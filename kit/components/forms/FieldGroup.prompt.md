FieldGroup — Nuxt UI 4.11.1 `UFieldGroup`; classes come straight from themes/field-group.ts.

```jsx
<FieldGroup size="xs" orientation="horizontal">Content</FieldGroup>
```

Variants: `size` (xs | sm | md | lg | xl), `orientation` (horizontal | vertical)

Override any slot with `ui={{ slot: "classes" }}`. Overlays take `open` as a prop — no behaviour.
